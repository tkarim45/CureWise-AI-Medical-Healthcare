# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Everyday patients and health-curious people, not clinicians. They arrive anxious
or simply curious: someone holding a blood-test PDF they don't understand, a
person with a skin or eye concern who wants a first read before booking a doctor,
a caregiver checking symptoms. They are on a laptop or phone, often outside a
clinic, and want a clear, calm answer plus a nudge toward professional care. Low
medical literacy is the norm; the interface must never assume jargon.

## Product Purpose

CureWise is a personal AI health companion. One account gives a user a set of
self-serve tools: a medical chatbot grounded in a curated knowledge base, upload
and plain-language explanation of blood reports, image-based screening across
seven models (kidney, lymphoma, pneumonia, eye disease, breast-cancer imaging,
blood-cell type, AML markers), acne/skin analysis, per-condition specialist chat,
and an emergency nearby-hospital finder. Success is a user going from "I don't
understand this / I'm worried" to "I understand what this might mean and what to
do next" in one calm session, always with a clear disclaimer that this informs,
not diagnoses.

## Brand Personality

Calm, clinical-trustworthy, precise. Three words: reassuring, credible, clear.
It should feel like a competent clinician who explains things slowly and never
rushes you: quiet confidence, not salesmanship. The voice is plain and warm
without being cute. Every screen lowers anxiety.

## Anti-references

- **Generic health SaaS**: stock-blue palette, rounded cards, smiling
  stock-photo hero, "Your health, simplified" template.
- **Cold hospital / EMR**: sterile grey clinical software, dense data tables,
  government-portal density.
- **Toy-like / childish**: bright primaries, cartoon mascots, oversized playful
  shapes.
- **AI slop**: purple gradients, glassmorphism everywhere, gradient text, the
  generic AI-startup look.

## Design Principles

1. **Lower anxiety first.** Layout, spacing, and copy exist to calm. Never
   alarm; surface urgency only where clinically real, and pair it with a next step.
2. **Plain language always.** Explain, don't display. Medical terms come with
   analogies; results come with "what this means" and "what to do".
3. **Honest about limits.** The disclaimer is part of the product, not fine
   print. Every AI result states it informs, not diagnoses.
4. **Confidence through restraint.** Trust is signaled by precision and calm, not
   by decoration. One accent, generous whitespace, exact alignment.
5. **One task per screen.** Each tool does one thing well; never make an anxious
   user hunt.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.2 AA. Body text ≥4.5:1, large text ≥3:1, placeholders held to the same
bar. Full keyboard navigation and visible focus. `prefers-reduced-motion`
honored on every animation. Light and dark themes both meet contrast. Plain
language and generous touch targets for older and anxious users.
